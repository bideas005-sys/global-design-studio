Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between Vector Tracing and Graphic Re-Designing?

Vector tracing recreates your exact existing image into crisp scalable paths without changing the design. Graphic re-designing improves the layout, fonts, colors, and overall visual appeal to give your brand a modern upgrade.

Can you work if I only have a rough screenshot or old print photo?

Yes! Even if you lost your original source files, send us a clear photo, PDF, or screenshot along with any text changes, and we will rebuild it from scratch.

Will I get editable source files after the redesign?

Yes, you receive full editable files (.CDR, .AI, .PSD, or .PDF) along with web-ready PNG and JPG exports.
