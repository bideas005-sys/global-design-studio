Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between Vector Tracing and Graphic Re-Designing?

Vector tracing recreates your exact existing image into crisp scalable paths without altering the design. Graphic re-designing upgrades the layout, typography, colors, and visual hierarchy to give your brand a modern, professional look.

Can you work if I only have a rough screenshot or old printed copy?

Yes! Even if you lost your original design files, send us a clear photo, PDF, or screenshot along with your updated text or logo, and we will rebuild and modernize it from scratch.

Will I get full editable source files after the redesign?

Yes, you receive complete, layered editable source files (.AI, .PSD, .CDR, or print-ready .PDF with bleeds) along with web-optimized PNG and JPG exports.
