Frequently Asked Questions

Do you use auto-trace tools or manual tracing?

We do 100% manual pen-tool vector tracing. No automated software is used, ensuring clean anchor points, smooth curves, and print-ready precision.

Which file formats will I receive?

You will receive editable source files including CorelDRAW (.CDR), Adobe Illustrator (.AI), .EPS, .SVG, print-ready .PDF, and high-resolution transparent PNGs.

How fast is the turnaround time?

Standard logos and simple graphics are delivered within 6 to 24 hours. Complex illustrations or multi-page redesigns are quoted upfront with exact timelines.
