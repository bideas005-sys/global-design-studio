Frequently Asked Questions

Do you use auto-trace tools or 100% manual tracing?

We do 100% manual pen-tool vector tracing. No automated trace software is ever used, guaranteeing clean anchor points, closed paths, and crisp edges at any scale—from a business card to a highway billboard.

Which vector and source file formats will I receive?

You will receive a complete delivery package including Adobe Illustrator (.AI), .EPS, .SVG, CorelDRAW (.CDR), print-ready .PDF, and high-resolution 300 DPI transparent PNG files.

Can you match Pantone (PMS) or CMYK colors and fonts?

Yes! We identify and recreate exact typography and provide separate spot-color (Pantone PMS), CMYK, or RGB versions tailored to your print or digital specifications.

How fast is your turnaround for urgent international orders?

Standard logos and clean graphics are delivered within 6 to 24 hours (with rush 2–4 hour delivery available upon request). Complex illustrations are quoted upfront with exact timelines.
