Frequently Asked Questions

What types of PVC cards and finishes do you offer?

We print standard CR80 credit-card thickness waterproof PVC cards in both glossy and matte finishes—ideal for corporate employee IDs, school/college badges, gym memberships, and luxury visiting cards.

Can you design the card, brochure, or label if I don't have a print file?

Yes! Our in-house graphic designers will create a custom, press-ready layout—or recreate an exact copy of your existing printed sample—and share a digital proof for your approval before printing.

What is your minimum order quantity and delivery coverage?

We accommodate small-batch orders for startups as well as bulk corporate runs. We offer fast doorstep courier delivery across Lahore and all major cities in Pakistan, plus global digital pre-press services.
