Vector Tracing & Artwork Preparation

What is the difference between manual vector tracing and auto-tracing?

Automated tracing tools guess edges and create jagged lines, warped fonts, and hundreds of messy anchor points that cause vinyl cutters and screen printers to glitch. At Global Design & Print Studio, we redraw every curve, corner, and letter manually using the Pen Tool in Adobe Illustrator and CorelDRAW for razor-sharp, press-ready results.

Which file formats will I receive when my logo is vectorized?

Every completed vector order includes a complete master bundle: editable .AI (Adobe Illustrator), .EPS, .SVG (for web and cutting machines), .CDR (CorelDRAW, on request), a print-ready Vector PDF, and a high-resolution 300 DPI transparent PNG.

Turnaround Time & International Payments
