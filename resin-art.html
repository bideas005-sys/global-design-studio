Frequently Asked Questions

Can I customize the colors, names, and size of my resin piece?

Absolutely! Every resin nameplate, wall clock, tray, and corporate gift is handmade to order with your chosen color palette, metallic pigments, gold/silver flakes, and custom text or logo.

How long does a custom resin order take to craft?

Because premium epoxy resin requires multiple pouring and curing layers for a bubble-free, glass-like finish, custom orders typically take 4 to 7 business days before dispatch.

Do you ship resin art internationally and is packaging safe?

Yes! We ship across Pakistan as well as worldwide via tracked international couriers (DHL/FedEx). Each piece is wrapped in protective film, multi-layer bubble wrap, and reinforced export boxes.
