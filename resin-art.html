Frequently Asked Questions

Can I customize the colors, names, and size of my resin piece?

Absolutely! Every resin nameplate, wall clock, tray, and keychain is handmade to order with your chosen color palette, gold/silver flakes, and custom text.

How long does a custom resin order take to make?

Because epoxy resin requires proper pouring and curing layers for a glass-like finish, custom orders typically take 4 to 7 days before dispatch.

Is shipping safe for fragile resin artwork?

Yes, we use multi-layer bubble wrap and sturdy protective boxes to ensure your resin art arrives safely without any scratches or damage.
